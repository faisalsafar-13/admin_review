UK rules to keep in mind
  • Probation is reviewed at week 12 of the 40-week year. Weeks count term time, but the legal clock runs on calendar months, holidays included.
  • From 1 January 2027, staff can claim unfair dismissal after 6 months' service (currently 2 years). Make the probation decision, including any extension, before the 6-month date shown in section 1.
  • Extending probation does not delay that protection. After 6 months, a dismissal needs a fair reason and a fair process.
  • Discrimination and whistleblowing protection apply from day one. Rate on evidence only.
  • Raise concerns early, in writing, with support and a fair chance to improve. Consider reasonable adjustments for a disability.
  • Do not count absence linked to disability, pregnancy or family leave against the employee.
